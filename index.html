I am a PhD student in the Computational Intelligence, Vision, and Robotics Lab (CILVR) at NYU Courant, where I am lucky to be advised by Lerrel Pinto. My research focuses on data-driven dexterous manipulation for multi-fingered robot hands. Since task-specific robot data is hard to collect with multi-fingered hands, my work centers on three topics:

  1. Learning by watching humans: AINA, HuDOR, HUG
  2. Humanoid hand hardware and control that best mimic human hands: RUKA, RUKA-v2
  3. Data-efficient multi-fingered policy learning: T-Dex, TAVI

I have a master's degree in Computer Science from NYU Courant and was awarded a Fulbright scholarship in 2020. I am currently a Research Scientist intern at Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics (FAR) and previously interned as a Research Scientist on Meta's Embodied AI team. Earlier, I worked as a full-time robotics engineer at DOF Robotics for a year, interned at X (the Moonshot Factory), and did two software engineering internships at Google. Previously, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from Bogazici University in Istanbul, Turkey, where I worked with Emre Ugur.

In my free time, I enjoy spending time with my , crafting, and playing muggle Quidditch! :)
